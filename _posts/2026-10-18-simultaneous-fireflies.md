In this assignment, I programmed three sets of LEDs to complete three loop functions. The first set of LEDs is the Big Dipper. These lights flash every 3 seconds, starting at second 0. The Big Dipper LEDs increase in brightness by a degree of 30 every 0.1 seconds, until they reach maximum brightness. They then turn off, and turn on again after another 3 seconds. The second set of LEDs is the Flash Bulb. These lights flash brightly for 0.5 seconds, starting at second 0, and repeat the cycle every 4 seconds. Their brightness does not fluctuate. The last set of LEDs is the Blue Ghost. These lights don't flash, but sustain a glow that fluctuates randomly between a low and high level of brightness. Every 0.5 seconds, the brightness of their glow randomly increases or decreases by a value of 50.

For others working on this project for the first time, I'd recommend labeling as you code. It was super helpful to have the variables, setup code, and loop code individually defined for the Big Dipper, the Flash Bulb, and the BLue Ghost lights.
