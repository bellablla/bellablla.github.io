I'm Isabella, and this semester, I'm taking Electronic Textiles with Professor Howley. In this course, student develop wearable technology devices, using both programming skills and technical sewing skills to create tactile projects. I love the fact that we're not only creating websites or digital projects, but rather objects that we can hold in our hands. I love fiber arts, and have experience with knitting and needlepoint. I'm also excited to create paper prototypes of circuits, and to learn how circuits work. I'm also looking forward to program an Arduino that can respond to analog and digital inputs and outputs. I'm also taking this course to fulfill my quantitative reasoning requirement, and I'm glad this course is being offered within the new Program in Computing.
